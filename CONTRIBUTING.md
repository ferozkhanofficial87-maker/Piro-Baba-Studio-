# 🤝 Contributing to Piro Baba Studio

Thank you for helping Piro Baba Studio preserve and share history.

We welcome contributions related to historical photographs, videos, research, corrections, sources, and heritage documentation.

## 📸 Historical Photos & Videos

If you would like to contribute historical material, please provide whenever possible:

- Location
- Approximate or exact date
- Photographer or original source
- Description of the historical scene
- Archive, book, website, or collection reference
- Any known copyright or usage information
